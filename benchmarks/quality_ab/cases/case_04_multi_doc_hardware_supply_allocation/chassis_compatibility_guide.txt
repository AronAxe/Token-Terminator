CHASSIS MECHANICAL AND ELECTRICAL COMPATIBILITY SPECIFICATION
Model: TitanBlade-4U-Modular
Target Enclosure: Rack-H-07 Slot-4
============================================================

PHYSICAL FORM FACTOR LIMITS:
- Maximum card slot width: 2 PCIe slots (Dual-Slot form factor only). 3-slot or triple-width cards are mechanically blocked by adjacent NVLink bridge shroud.
- Maximum card length: 310 mm.
- Maximum card height: Standard full-height 111.15 mm.

ELECTRICAL CONNECTOR SPECIFICATIONS:
- Required power delivery: Dual 12V-2x6 PCIe Gen5 16-pin connectors (supporting up to 600W per cable + 75W PCIe slot).
- Total slot maximum safe power delivery: 1800W sustained.

Model 'NeuralJet-800': Dual-slot (COMPATIBLE), length 290mm (COMPATIBLE).
Model 'MatrixCore-Ultra': Triple-slot (INCOMPATIBLE - physically blocked by TitanBlade 4U shroud).
Model 'A800-DualSlot': Dual-slot (COMPATIBLE), length 300mm (COMPATIBLE), dual 12V-2x6 connectors (COMPATIBLE), TDP 1650W.
Model 'LiteAcc-300': Single-slot (COMPATIBLE).
